How to evaluate possible opportunities

Research checklist—not a buy signal
SHORT TERM · 1 MONTH
Momentum & volatility
Look at recent price movement, trading volume, upcoming earnings, and how much the price normally fluctuates.
MEDIUM TERM · 6 MONTHS
Growth & valuation
Compare revenue and earnings growth with valuation, company guidance, competitors, and sector performance.
LONG TERM · 1 YEAR+
Business fundamentals
Consider cash flow, debt, competitive position, dilution, economic risks, and whether growth assumptions are realistic.
